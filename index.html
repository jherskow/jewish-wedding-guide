The groom's friends sing around him while the ketubah, the marriage contract, is signed.

As in 'deck the halls'. The groom veils the bride. The New York licence is signed here.

The canopy stands for the couple's home. When the glass breaks, shout 'mazel tov'.

The couple's first minutes alone. Guests go to dinner.

Courses alternate with dancing. Get up when the music starts.

No steps to learn. It starts with men and women on either side of a divider, the mechitza, then the floor is mixed.
